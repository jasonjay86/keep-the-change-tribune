Rest-of-season points only, in half-PPR terms, counted from the week after the deal. Picks are scored as the player eventually taken with them, in the same window, and only for the weeks that player was on the acquiring roster — which is why a pick's points can land in a later season than the trade itself. A pick not yet used is TBD and freezes the whole verdict. A trade also needs three weeks of returns before it earns one — until then it reads "too recent to judge" and is left out of career trade margins. Two further trades are excluded: Sleeper's roster numbering on those records does not match the weekly rosters, so the returns cannot be verified.
